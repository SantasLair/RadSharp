# Changelog

RadSharp uses [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/). Until 1.0, previews (`0.x.y-preview.n`) may change APIs.
Each release's full notes are in [docs/releases/](docs/releases/).

## 0.1.0-preview.1

The first preview: the designer (standalone and in Visual Studio), F9 live run, about 40 Skia-drawn controls, form
files and the source generator, `Binder<T>` and `Action`, per-monitor DPI, and NativeAOT publishing with packaging for
Windows, macOS and Linux. See [docs/releases/0.1.0-preview.1.md](docs/releases/0.1.0-preview.1.md) and
[docs/known-limits.md](docs/known-limits.md).
